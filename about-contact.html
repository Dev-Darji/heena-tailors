About Heena Ladies Tailor

A local women's tailoring studio committed to comfortable fitting and personal designs.

Our Story

Dedicated to Good Fitting & Personal Style

Heena Ladies Tailor is a women's tailoring studio focused on custom stitching, good fitting and designs made to suit each customer. Whether you have your own design or need help choosing a style, we are here to help you create an outfit you will feel comfortable wearing.

We understand that no two bodies and no two styling preferences are the same. That is why we pay close attention to your measurements, neckline depth, sleeve length, and fabric drape before cutting and stitching.

How We Work

Our Approach

Three simple principles at the heart of every outfit we stitch.

01 / Choice

Your Choice

Your design, your preferences. We work with your ideas, screenshots, or sample clothes so you get the look you want.

02 / Fit

Your Fit

Measurements taken with care. We ensure armholes, shoulders, and waistlines fit naturally without tight pulling.

03 / Craft

Our Work

Neat stitching and finishing. Sturdy seams, smooth zippers, even hem borders, and quality thread work that lasts.
