Tailored to You

You Choose the Style

Every woman has her own comfort and styling preference. When you visit or message us, you can customize every detail of your outfit:

•

Neck Design

Round, V-neck, boat neck, collar neck, sweet heart, square, or keyhole.

•

Sleeve Style

Sleeveless, cap, short, elbow-length, 3/4th, full sleeves, puff, or bell sleeves.

•

Length

Short, knee-length, calf-length, or floor-length tailored to your height.

•

Fit

Comfortable loose fit, regular fit, or structured slim fit as preferred.

•

Fabric

Cotton, silk, organza, georgette, chiffon, velvet, linen, and brocade.

•

Finishing

Piping, borders, laces, handmade buttons, latkans, and lining choices.

•

Overall Design

Bring reference pictures from Pinterest, Instagram, or family albums.

•

Fitting Trials

Trial fitting before final delivery to ensure zero discomfort.

Feel free to bring your fabric and references to our shop or send them directly on WhatsApp to discuss what works best for you.
